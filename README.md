# 💫 Hi, I'm Gabriela Ojeda Semidey
**Product Owner | Service Designer | UX Researcher**

### From Discovery to Delivery.

I'm a Product Owner who turns complex problems into clear, high-impact products. I work at the intersection of **business, users, and technology** — prioritizing what generates real value.

---

## 👩🏻‍💻 About Me

I come from the strategic design world: **5+ years designing products and services** for organizations like Clínica Alemana, the IDB and Spotify. Today I lead teams from discovery to delivery as a Product Owner.

Before defining any feature, I ask myself: *does this solve a real user problem and generate business value?* My background in **Service Design and UX Research** lets me go beyond the backlog — I identify root causes, map the systems behind problems, and make decisions with data and context.

I understand developers because I know what it's like to build. I understand users because I've done research for years. And I understand the business because I've always worked aligning both. **That triangle is where I live.**

- 🧭 **Product Vision** — roadmaps and backlogs prioritized by user value, technical feasibility and business impact
- 🔍 **Research & Discovery** — 80+ user interviews, usability testing, data analysis
- 🌉 **Bridge Between Worlds** — acceptance criteria that dev teams actually understand
- 🤝 **Facilitation & Alignment** — workshops and design sprints that get stakeholders, design, dev and business on the same page
